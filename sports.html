Sports & Interests

What I do outside the classroom – and what it says about me.

Sports & activities

I have practised four very different sports – one team sport and three disciplines built on precision, concentration and self-control – as well as theatre.

Handball

Team sport, with the N7 sports association

A fast, physical team sport where every action depends on communication and quick collective decisions. It taught me team spirit and how to stay focused under pressure. I have been playing for three years, mostly as a pivot, and sometimes as goalkeeper. I now play with the N7 handball team.

Sport shooting

Precision sport

I started in 2025–2026 as a licensed shooter, using semi-automatic pistols and revolvers on targets at up to 25 m. I shoot for training, and to unwind and recharge. Sport shooting is all about precision, breath control and strict respect of safety rules. It requires calm, rigour and the ability to repeat the same gesture perfectly.

Climbing

Individual sport, with the N7 sports association

Climbing is like solving a problem with your whole body: you read the route, plan your moves and manage your effort. It builds perseverance and trust with your belay partner. I have been climbing for six years, mostly indoors at a 5c–6a level, with some climbing in the mountains. I now climb with the N7 climbing section.

Fencing

Combat sport

Fencing combines speed, strategy and respect for the opponent. Each bout is a quick duel of anticipation, which trains reflexes and decision-making. I fenced foil for two years in high school.

Theatre

Stage activity

I did theatre for three years. Being on stage taught me to speak in front of an audience and to feel at ease when presenting my work.

Interests

Science & technology

I am passionate about aeronautics and embedded systems, two fields at the heart of modern defence equipment and of the career I want to build in the defence sector. I also follow the global defence industry, including emerging players such as China.

Culture

I am passionate about classical music, from Beethoven to Rachmaninoff, and above all about opera. I also love museums and art in general: a way of capturing life and nature in a moment that is frozen, yet still alive.

Other

Photography and cinema, which share that same way of capturing a moment.
