Civic Engagement

Serving others and the community – a value I want to put at the heart of my career in defence.

Why it matters to me

Working for national defence means working for the common good. I believe this commitment starts now, through concrete actions at school and in society.

The defence industry is part of my family's history: my grandfather worked at Matra, and my father also worked in the defence sector.

I was declared unfit for military service, so I will not be able to serve my country in uniform. I still want to help, and that is one of the reasons why I want to work at the DGA: as an engineer, I can serve national defence in another way.

Student associations at N7

CAn7

Member in training · Stage and audio

CAn7 is the N7 student association that provides sound, lighting and DJs for events such as school galas, parties, concerts and conferences, with more than 25 years of experience.

I joined the association and am currently training for the roles related to setting up the stage and to audio: placing the microphones and processing the sound.
