Mobility

Defence programmes are international: working abroad is a key step to becoming a well-rounded engineer.

Why international mobility?

Major defence programmes are increasingly developed through European and NATO cooperation (joint missile, radar, naval and aircraft programmes). Engineers in this field need to work in English, understand other engineering cultures and build networks abroad. ENSEEIHT, like French engineering schools in general, also requires at least 17 weeks abroad to graduate, through an academic exchange, an internship or a double degree.

I am aiming for countries that are major players in defence beyond France, such as the United States, the United Kingdom and Germany.
