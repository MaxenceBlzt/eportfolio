About me

Welcome to my ePortfolio! I am a first-year student in the 3EA department (Electronics, Electrical Energy and Automatic Control) at ENSEEIHT, the electrical engineering school of Toulouse INP, also known as "N7".

I joined N7 through the competitive CCINP entrance exam, after two years of intensive preparatory classes (classes préparatoires, PSI track – Physics and Engineering Sciences) at Lycée Chaptal in Paris. Those two years gave me solid foundations in physics and mathematics, my two main strengths, as well as the work ethic needed to tackle demanding technical problems.

My long-term goal is to work on the systems that protect our country: I would like to become an engineer in the defence industry (Thales, MBDA, Safran, Naval Group…) and, ideally, one day join the corps of armament engineers (IETA) of the French Defence Procurement Agency (DGA). Energy conversion, embedded electronics and control systems are at the heart of modern defence equipment, which is why I chose the 3EA department.
