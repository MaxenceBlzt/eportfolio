Role models

Three professionals whose career paths inspire my own project. Each one is linked to one of my international mobility projects.

Role model 1

Emeric Crinon

Former engineer · DGA – French Defence Procurement Agency, French submarines

Career path
Graduate of the École navale (the French Naval Academy), then engineer at the DGA, working on French submarines.
How I found this person
A friend of my family. We have had many one-on-one conversations over the years. As time went by, I understood and absorbed more of what he told me, which allowed me to build my knowledge of the subject.
Key takeaway for my project
Emeric is the person who made me want to become an engineer and to work in national defence. He taught me how France maintains its submarines and designs the next generation, and which technologies will shape the future of our naval industry, such as greater use of AI and new electrical systems. The physics of fluids does not change, but these technologies evolve every year, and that is where the difference, and therefore victory, is decided. His lesson, which I intend to apply throughout my career: always stay up to date on computing, digital and electrical technologies.

Role model 2

Pascale de Laitre

Account Executive · SAP

Career path
During her studies in electronics and robotics, she worked for American companies in electronics and robotics research and in automation, before coming back to work in France. After a double degree in business, she moved into sales, where she uses her scientific knowledge: she has been an Account Executive at SAP since 2008. She also has knowledge of the defence sector.
How I found this person
A friend of my family. We have had many one-on-one conversations over the years.
Key takeaway for my project
She helped me choose the 3EA track at N7 by showing me why electronics matters: it is and will remain indispensable, and it is becoming more and more interesting with the use of AI. She also made me see that engineers need communication and people skills, because they must be able to explain their product and the market it targets to business people. Finally, she showed me the role an engineer can play at the DGA in protecting national sovereignty, as AI is used to design new technologies.

Role model 3

Éric Béranger

CEO of MBDA, 2019–2026

Career path
A graduate of École Polytechnique and Télécom Paris, he began his career in 1988 and worked at France Télécom, Société Générale and Matra Marconi Space. He then became CEO of Astrium Services in 2003 and of Astrium Satellites in 2012, and later CEO of OneWeb, before leading MBDA from 2019. He is also a colonel in the French Air Force reserve.
How I found this person
I have never met him. I discovered his career through my own research on the defence industry.
Key takeaway for my project
What inspires me is his path of excellence, which led him to serve French defence and the armaments industry through his work.
