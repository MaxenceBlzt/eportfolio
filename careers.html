Role models

Three professionals whose career paths inspire my own project. Each one is linked to one of my international mobility projects.

Role model 1

Emeric Crinon

Former engineer · DGA – French Defence Procurement Agency, French submarines

Career path
Graduate of the École navale (the French Naval Academy), then engineer at the DGA, working on French submarines.
How I found this person
A friend of my family. We have had many one-on-one conversations over the years. As time went by, I understood and absorbed more of what he told me, which allowed me to build my knowledge of the subject.
Key takeaway for my project
Emeric is the person who made me want to become an engineer and to work in national defence. He taught me how France maintains its submarines and designs the next generation, and which technologies will shape the future of our naval industry, such as greater use of AI and new electrical systems. The physics of fluids does not change, but these technologies evolve every year, and that is where the difference, and therefore victory, is decided. His lesson, which I intend to apply throughout my career: always stay up to date on computing, digital and electrical technologies.
